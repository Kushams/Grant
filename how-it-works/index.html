The applicant journey

How it works

From discovering an opportunity to understanding a decision — what to expect at each step.

Applying does not guarantee approval or payment

Applying does not guarantee approval, funding or payment. Any applicable charges, if any, will be stated in the verified programme terms before you proceed.
  1. 1

    Step 1: Discover an opportunity

    Search the directory and filter by who can apply, category, region, deadline and funding.

  2. 2

    Step 2: Read the eligibility criteria

    Every opportunity lists who can apply, where, what you need to provide and how decisions are made.

  3. 3

    Step 3: Create an account

    Register as an individual or as an organization or business. It takes a minute and does not commit you to anything.

  4. 4

    Step 4: Complete the appropriate profile

    Add the basics about you or your organization. We never ask for ID numbers, bank details or card data.

  5. 5

    Step 5: Prepare and submit the application

    Work through a guided, multi-step form. Progress is saved as a draft so you can leave and return.

  6. 6

    Step 6: Receive a reference number

    On submission you receive a unique reference such as NEN-APP-2026-ABC234 and an in-app confirmation.

  7. 7

    Step 7: Track the review process

    Follow your status and a timeline of every change from your dashboard.

  8. 8

    Step 8: Respond to any requests for further information

    Reviewers may ask a question or request a revision. You reply or edit from your application page.

  9. 9

    Step 9: Receive a decision

    A final decision is recorded by an administrator, separately from the reviewer’s recommendation.

  10. 10

    Step 10: If approved, review the confirmed award terms

    Staff may record an award and a planned disbursement schedule, which you can view on your application page.

How applications are reviewed

  1. Initial review — completeness and basic eligibility.
  2. Detailed review — a reviewer assesses the application against the programme criteria and may ask for more information.
  3. Recommendation — the reviewer records a recommendation. This is not a decision.
  4. Final decision — an administrator approves or declines, with a recorded reason.

Internal reviewer notes are confidential and are not shown to applicants.
