Frequently asked questions

Straight answers about applying, tracking and staying safe.

It depends on the opportunity. Each programme states which applicant types it accepts — for example individuals, entrepreneurs, small or registered businesses, nonprofits or community organizations — and which countries or regions are eligible. Always read the eligibility section on the opportunity page before you start.

Use the opportunity directory to search by keyword and filter by applicant type, category, region, status, deadline or funding amount. Each opportunity page shows its status and anything still to be confirmed.

Yes. Applications are saved as drafts as you move between steps. Sign in again at any time and choose “Continue your draft” — you will resume at the last step you saved. Your application is not submitted until you confirm on the final step.

After you submit, you receive a reference number. Your dashboard and the application page show the current status, a timeline of every change, any messages or information requests from reviewers, and — if an award is later recorded — the award and planned instalments.

A reviewer checks completeness and eligibility, then assesses the application against the programme’s criteria. Reviewers can record a recommendation, but a separate administrator records the final decision. Internal reviewer notes are never shown to applicants.

A reviewer can ask for additional information (you reply from your application page) or ask you to revise and resubmit. You will see a notification and the exact request. Your application stays open while you respond.

No. Applying does not guarantee approval, funding or payment. Funding decisions depend on each programme’s verified terms, eligibility and available resources.

If an application is approved, staff may record an award and a planned schedule of instalments. You will see these on your application page and receive an in-app notification. Statuses such as “Paid — manually recorded” are entries made by authorized staff; this platform does not move money or verify bank transfers.

Any applicable charges, if any, will be stated in the verified programme terms before you proceed. No fees are charged to apply. Be very cautious about anyone asking for a payment to apply, to “release” or “unlock” funds, or to guarantee approval.

Warning signs include requests for upfront payments or gift cards, pressure to act immediately, requests for passwords, one-time codes or bank details, guaranteed approval, and messages from personal email or messaging accounts. Read our fraud-prevention guidance and report anything suspicious to support.

Use the contact form. Messages are stored in a support queue for staff and replies appear in the site; no phone number or office address is published until the organization confirms them.

We aim to collect only what is needed to review an application, and we never ask for government ID numbers, bank details or payment-card data. Our privacy notice is currently a draft (DRAFT-0.1 (pending approval)) awaiting organizational approval.
