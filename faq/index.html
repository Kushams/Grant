FAQ & staying safe

Straight answers about applying, and how to protect yourself from fake offers.

Individuals, entrepreneurs and small businesses, creatives, community groups and nonprofits anywhere in the world, except applicants located in Africa. Every application is reviewed against the published criteria, and not every applicant will qualify.

Applications open on 1 November 2026. The application form will be on the General Opportunity Fund page. It asks who you are, what the funding is for, the amount you are requesting (between $25,000 and $500,000) and a short budget.

No. Applying does not guarantee approval, funding or payment. The Board of Directors decides each application and may approve the amount requested, a different amount, or decline.

Any applicable charges, if any, will be stated in the verified programme terms before you proceed. No fees are charged to apply or to receive an award. Never pay an outside agent to “guarantee” a grant, and be very cautious about anyone asking for a payment to “release” or “unlock” funds.

Approved awards are paid by cheque to the approved recipient, after written approval and acceptance of the written award terms.

Email info@nen.org or use the contact form. info@nen.org is our only official email address.

Warning signs include requests for upfront payments or gift cards, pressure to act immediately, requests for passwords, one-time codes or bank details, guaranteed approval, and messages from personal accounts or messaging apps claiming to be an official body.

We collect only what is needed to review an application, and we never ask for Social Security numbers, ID images, bank details or payment-card data. See the privacy notice for details.

Staying safe

Genuine funding programmes do not charge you to receive money.

Red flags

  • Requests for an upfront payment — to “release”, “unlock”, “clear”, “insure” or “deliver” funds, or to “guarantee” approval.
  • Any outside agent who promises or guarantees a grant for a fee.
  • Requests for gift cards, cryptocurrency, wire transfers or mobile-money to a personal account.
  • Requests for your passwords, one-time codes or PINs, or for unnecessary sensitive data such as ID numbers or bank statements.
  • Promises of guaranteed funding, or pressure to act within hours.
  • Messages from free email accounts or social media claiming to be an official body.

Our commitments

  • Applying is free, and we never ask you to pay a fee to receive an award.
  • This site has no checkout or payment page and never asks for card, bank, wallet or cryptocurrency details.
  • We will not ask for sensitive information by social media or personal messaging apps.

If you are targeted

  1. Stop communicating and do not send money or personal data.
  2. Keep screenshots and any reference numbers.
  3. Tell us through the contact form (topic “Reporting a suspicious offer”) and report it to your local consumer-protection or police authority.
  4. If you shared a password, change it everywhere you use it. If you paid, contact your bank immediately.
