An independent empowerment and opportunity-access platform that helps individuals, entrepreneurs, businesses and organizations discover programmes, understand eligibility and track their applications.

National Empowerment Network is an independent organization. It is not a government agency, and nothing on this site is an official government programme.

Accessibility

Accessibility statement

We want everyone to be able to find and apply for opportunities.

Pending formal approval

This page is awaiting formal approval by National Empowerment Network and legal review (version: DRAFT-0.1 (pending approval)). Legal entity details, registration numbers, governing law, official addresses and privacy contacts are intentionally left blank until supplied and verified.

Our aim

We are building this platform to follow WCAG 2.2 level AA practices wherever feasible. This is an aim, not a certification: this platform has not yet had a formal third-party accessibility audit.

What we have built in

  • Semantic page structure, a “skip to main content” link and clearly labelled navigation landmarks.
  • Full keyboard operation with visible focus indicators.
  • Labelled form fields, clear validation messages and screen-reader announcements for results.
  • Colour contrast chosen to meet AA, and status shown with text — never colour alone.
  • Responsive layouts for mobile, tablet and desktop, and tables that scroll rather than break.
  • Reduced-motion support.

Known limitations

  • No formal audit or assistive-technology test matrix has been completed yet.
  • Some dashboard tables are wide and rely on horizontal scrolling on small screens.

Tell us about a barrier

If something is hard to use, please tell us through the contact form and choose the topic “Accessibility”. We will record it in the support queue. No official accessibility contact address has been confirmed yet.
