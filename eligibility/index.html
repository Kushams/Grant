Eligibility

Eligibility guide

Eligibility differs from one programme to the next. This guide explains the categories you will see — the opportunity page is always the authority.

Not every category qualifies for every opportunity

Each opportunity lists the applicant types, regions and criteria it accepts. These categories are configurable by administrators, so the list below is a general guide, not a promise of eligibility.

  • Individuals

    People applying in their own name.

    May be asked about location and the purpose of their request.

  • Entrepreneurs

    Individuals building or launching a venture who may not yet have a registered business.

    May be asked to describe the venture and its stage.

  • Small businesses

    Small enterprises, registered or not, depending on the programme.

    Programmes may set size, sector or location conditions.

  • Registered businesses

    Businesses that are formally registered where they operate.

    Registration requirements are set per programme — none are assumed here.

  • Nonprofit organizations

    Not-for-profit organizations, including NGOs.

    Programmes may set mission, location or governance conditions.

  • Community organizations

    Local groups, cooperatives and associations.

    Programmes may accept informal groups or require formal status.

  • Other eligible entities

    Any other entity type an administrator configures for a specific programme.

    Defined entirely by that programme’s terms.

What programmes typically check

  • Whether your applicant type is accepted.
  • Whether your country or region is eligible.
  • Whether the application window is open (opening date and deadline).
  • Whether you have already applied, where a programme allows only one application each.
  • Programme-specific questions and any required information.

If an item is not yet confirmed for an opportunity you will see “Details to be confirmed” — we never fill gaps with guesses. We do not require identity documents, bank statements or payment details in this prototype.
