Stay safe

Applying is free

Any applicable charges, if any, will be stated in the verified programme terms before you proceed.

How to spot a fake offer

  • Clear status

    Everything about the Fund is on one page, with what is still to be confirmed.

  • No sensitive data

    We never ask for ID numbers, bank details or card data.

  • Honest records

    Award and payment records are entered by staff and labelled as such.

  • No upfront payments

    Never pay an outside agent to “guarantee” a grant.

Frequently asked questions

Individuals, entrepreneurs and small businesses, creatives, community groups and nonprofits anywhere in the world, except applicants located in Africa. Every application is reviewed against the published criteria, and not every applicant will qualify.

Applications are open. Use the short application form on the General Opportunity Fund page — no account is needed. It asks who you are, what the funding is for, the amount you are requesting (between $25,000 and $500,000) and a short budget. You will be given a reference (for example NEN-APP-2026-ABC234) when you submit; keep it safe. We contact you about the outcome using the details you give.

No. Applying does not guarantee approval, funding or payment. The Board of Directors decides each application and may approve the amount requested, a different amount, or decline.

Any applicable charges, if any, will be stated in the verified programme terms before you proceed. No fees are charged to apply or to receive an award. Never pay an outside agent to “guarantee” a grant, and be very cautious about anyone asking for a payment to “release” or “unlock” funds.
