Stay safe

Applying is free

Any applicable charges, if any, will be stated in the verified programme terms before you proceed.

How to spot a fake offer

  • Clear status

    Every opportunity shows whether it is open, upcoming or closed.

  • No sensitive data

    We never ask for ID numbers, bank details or card data.

  • Honest records

    Award and payment records are entered by staff and labelled as such.

  • No upfront payments

    Never pay an outside agent to “guarantee” a grant.
