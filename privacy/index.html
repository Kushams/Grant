Privacy notice

How this platform handles personal information.

Pending formal approval

This page is awaiting formal approval by National Empowerment Network and legal review (version: DRAFT-0.1 (pending approval)). Legal entity details, registration numbers, governing law, official addresses and privacy contacts are intentionally left blank until supplied and verified.

Who is responsible

National Empowerment Network, LLC is the controller of data collected through this platform. Privacy questions and requests: info@nen.org. Registration details and a postal address will be added when confirmed.

What we collect

  • Application form: the details you enter — such as your name, contact details, country, what the funding is for, the amount you request and a short budget — and your consent record.
  • Support messages you send us through the contact form or by email.
  • Staff records: notes and the status of your application, and any award record, entered by authorized staff.

What we do not collect

We do not ask for government ID numbers, tax numbers, bank account details, payment-card data, wallet or cryptocurrency details, or passwords for other services. This platform does not currently accept file uploads.

How we use it

  • To receive and review your application and to contact you about it, using the details you gave us.
  • To let authorized staff review applications and record decisions.
  • To keep an audit trail of sensitive actions for accountability and security.

Who can see it

Only authorized National Empowerment Network staff can see what you submit. We do not sell personal data.

Notifications

The platform currently sends no emails or SMS. Notifications appear only inside the site.

Retention, rights and contact

Retention periods, your rights under applicable law and the privacy contact are to be confirmed by the organization and legal counsel before any public launch.

Version: DRAFT-0.1 (pending approval)

An independent empowerment and opportunity-access platform that helps individuals, entrepreneurs, businesses and organizations discover programmes, understand eligibility and track their applications.

National Empowerment Network is an independent organization. It is not a government agency, and nothing on this site is an official government programme.

Applying does not guarantee approval, funding or payment. Any applicable charges, if any, will be stated in the verified programme terms before you proceed.
