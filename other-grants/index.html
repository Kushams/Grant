Other grants

Programmes run by other organizations. NEN does not offer, fund or decide these — we point you to the official source.

Read this first

Each programme has its own rules and its own official application. Many grants are for organizations rather than individuals. Never pay an outside agent to “guarantee” a grant — official programmes do not charge to apply. If anyone asks you for money, first confirm with that programme’s own customer service, using the contact details on its official site, before paying anything.

Education

  • U.S. Department of Education — Federal Student Aid

    Federal Pell Grant

    Grants for undergraduate students with financial need. They generally do not have to be repaid.

  • U.S. Department of Education — Federal Student Aid

    Federal Supplemental Educational Opportunity Grant (FSEOG)

    Grants for undergraduates with exceptional financial need, awarded through participating schools.

  • U.S. Department of Education — Federal Student Aid

    TEACH Grant

    Grants for students preparing to teach in high-need fields at schools serving low-income families. It comes with a service requirement.

Healthcare

  • U.S. Health Resources & Services Administration (HRSA)

    Health Center Program

    Funding for community health centers that provide care in underserved communities.

Family & housing

  • U.S. Administration for Children and Families — Office of Head Start

    Head Start

    Early childhood education and family support for children from low-income families.

  • U.S. Administration for Children and Families — Office of Community Services

    Low Income Home Energy Assistance Program (LIHEAP)

    Help with home heating and cooling costs. Applications go through state, tribal or local agencies.

  • U.S. Administration for Children and Families — Office of Family Assistance

    Temporary Assistance for Needy Families (TANF)

    Temporary cash assistance and services for families with children, run through state programmes.

  • U.S. Department of Agriculture — Food and Nutrition Service

    WIC (Special Supplemental Nutrition Program for Women, Infants, and Children)

    Nutrition support for pregnant and postpartum women, infants and young children, through state agencies.

  • U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development (HUD)

    Housing Choice Voucher Program

    Rental assistance through local public housing agencies.

  • U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development (HUD)

    Community Development Block Grants (CDBG)

    Grants to states and local governments for community development. Individuals usually reach these through local government programmes.

Small business

  • U.S. Small Business Administration

    SBA grants and funding programs

    The SBA’s overview of grants and other funding programmes for small businesses and organizations.

  • America’s Seed Fund (SBIR.gov)

    SBIR / STTR

    Federal funding for small businesses doing research and development.

Arts & humanities

  • National Endowment for the Arts

    National Endowment for the Arts Grants

    Grants for arts projects, organizations and communities.

  • National Endowment for the Humanities

    National Endowment for the Humanities Grants

    Grants for humanities projects, scholarship, education and public programmes.

Research

  • National Institutes of Health

    NIH Grants and Funding

    Funding for biomedical and health research, mainly for researchers and institutions.

  • National Science Foundation

    NSF Funding

    Funding for research and education in science and engineering.

Find more grants

  • U.S. federal government

    Grants.gov

    The central site for finding and applying for federal grant opportunities. Most are for organizations rather than individuals.

  • USAGov (U.S. General Services Administration)

    USAGov: Government grants, loans and benefits

    A plain-language guide to government grants and benefits, with links to the official programmes.
