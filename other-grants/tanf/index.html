Temporary Assistance for Needy Families (TANF)

Temporary cash assistance and services for families with children, run through state programmes.

This grant is offered by U.S. Administration for Children and Families — Office of Family Assistance, not by National Empowerment Network

National Empowerment Network does not run, fund, endorse or decide this programme, and cannot tell you whether you qualify. To apply you will go to the official site, where you register and apply with them directly.
Offered by

  • Apply only on the official site. Official programmes do not charge to apply.
  • Never pay an outside agent who promises or “guarantees” this grant. If anyone asks for a payment, confirm with this programme’s own customer service, using the contact details on its official site, before paying anything.
  • Do not send personal documents to anyone who contacted you first.
  • Check the address in your browser matches the official page above.
